# Privacy Policy — Oura Coach (Beta)

**Effective date:** 2026-01-04

Oura Coach (Beta) is a personal wellness application that helps you view and interpret your Oura data. It is not a medical application and does not provide medical advice.

## Information We Collect
When you connect your Oura account, we may access and process:
- Oura account identifiers (e.g., basic profile info, depending on scopes you grant)
- Sleep, readiness, and activity metrics (depending on scopes you grant)
- Optional user-provided notes (e.g., caffeine, alcohol, late meals, workouts)

## OAuth Tokens
To access your Oura data, the backend stores OAuth tokens (including refresh tokens) securely. The iOS app does not store your client secret.

## How We Use Your Data
We use your data to:
- Fetch and display your Oura metrics
- Generate wellness insights, trends, and experiments for you to try
- Improve reliability and performance (e.g., caching, debugging)

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your data.
We do not share your personal data with third parties for advertising.

We may use service providers for hosting (e.g., servers/databases) strictly to operate the app.

## Data Retention
We retain your data only as long as needed to provide the service.
You can request deletion of your account data at any time (see "Contact").

## Security
We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, but we aim to minimize risk.

## Your Choices
- You can revoke access to Oura data by disconnecting your Oura account.
- You can request deletion of stored data and tokens.

## Children's Privacy
This app is not intended for children under 13.

## Changes
We may update this policy. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date.
